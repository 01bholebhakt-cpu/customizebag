Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Design your bag, add it to your bag and place your order. It opens on WhatsApp ready to send, and we confirm the design, delivery and payment there.

Can I add any name?

Yes, up to 14 characters, in three lettering styles and five colours.

How long does it take?

Each bag is made to order. Estimated delivery is 5 to 8 working days after your design is confirmed.

How do I pay?

Payment details (UPI, bank transfer or a payment link) are shared on WhatsApp after we confirm your order.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. Shipping is free above ₹1,999, otherwise ₹79.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes, for weddings, bridesmaids and return gifts. Message us your quantity and date.

Can I change my design?

Yes, before we start making it. Message us as soon as possible.

Can I return a personalised bag?

We cannot accept returns for change of mind since each bag is made for you. If it arrives damaged or incorrect, message us within 48 hours.
