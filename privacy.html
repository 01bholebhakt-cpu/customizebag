Policies

Privacy policy

What we collect

When you order, you share your name, phone number and delivery address. Your bag and details are saved in your own browser so they are ready next time. They are not sent to a server.

How we use it

Your details are sent to us as a WhatsApp message only when you place an order. We use them to confirm, make and deliver your order.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. An address is shared with a courier only to deliver your order.

Your choices

Clear this site's data in your browser to remove saved details, or ask us on WhatsApp to delete your order details.
