Policies

Returns & refunds

Personalised bags

Because every bag is made to order with your name, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

Damaged or wrong items

If your bag arrives damaged, or the lettering does not match what you confirmed, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will repair, remake or refund as appropriate.

Cancellations

You can change or cancel before we begin making your bag. Message us as early as you can.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original mode of payment. We will confirm the timeline with you.
